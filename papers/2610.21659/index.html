Overview

Availability-time discipline targets leakage within a single event, which a chronological train/test split cannot fix.

Methods & research question

An Availability Contract records the model input boundary, evidence for when that input was usable, processing allowance, outcome start and permitted interpretation. For 552 earnings calls from 24 firms, the authors hold the return endpoint fixed while comparing previous-close, documentary call-start and audio-end-plus-15-minute completion-proxy start prices. A separate release-session test shifts volatility-target windows for four frozen prediction streams.

Original manuscript v1, Sections 1 and 3–5 (availability contract, three-clock audit and release-session sensitivity). This explanatory reading does not establish independent validation.

Evidence and judgement

Canonical source-report assessment: Abstract + metadata

Reader V1.2 separately inspected selected sections of the original arXiv manuscript HTML (v1) to prepare an explanatory note. The source report's earlier evidence classification remains unchanged; original author results are not independently replicated, and code, data, trading edge and execution claims remain unverified.

Findings reported by the authors

  • Moving from the documentary call-start proxy to the audio-completion proxy flips the reported return sign for 9.4% of the 552 calls; 28.3% change quintile when completion-clock cutpoints are held fixed.
  • In the independent release-session exercise, moving before-market three-day targets forward by one trading session raises reported mean squared error by 18.1–21.4% across four fixed prediction streams, even though their model rankings remain unchanged.

Source: original manuscript v1, Section 1, paired-clock results and independent release-session analysis. These are author-reported observations, not Quant Radar calculations.

Research significance (editorial interpretation)

Chronological splitting alone does not prevent a target interval from starting before a complete transcript could exist. Results can appear rank-stable while the absolute benchmark loss changes because the observable prediction clock changed.

Limitations & unresolved gates

The audio completion time plus processing allowance is a reconstruction/proxy, not verified real-time transcript readiness. Retrospectively constructed representations and label anchors are not trade arrival, execution, cost or deployability evidence. The revised timing also changes the estimand, not merely a nuisance parameter.

Research actions

For one historical call fixture, can an explicit available-at/processed-at/decision-at/outcome-from contract move the label start behind the documented complete-input time, then compare sign, category and loss against the original window? No live inference or trading is authorised.

This is a research question, not permission to perform an experiment or change screening, publication, backlog or trading state.
