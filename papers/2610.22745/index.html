Overview

A valuable cautionary design for frozen pre-origin language models. The authors report that the best validation-score model loses money after costs and their combination is not superior to a price-only comparator; checkpoints, news timestamps and cost arithmetic not verified.

Material limitations

Relevant costs, point-in-time controls, baselines, universe construction, portfolio sizing, turnover, liquidity/capacity, reproducibility and trading effect remain not independently verified at Abstract + metadata depth.
