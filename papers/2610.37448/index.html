Overview

The authors compare isolation and communication variants for 200 agents from four model families over seven US trading days and report convergence of positions and wording in some groups. The decisive point is their explicit no-peer baseline: correlated inputs can produce herding without message passing. Read for a testable diversification-risk protocol, not a trading-return advantage.
