Quant-Arena: Benchmarking LLM Agents for Quantitative Trading Research

Overview

Tests an entire agent research loop under a sealed assessment year rather than treating generated code or an attractive training backtest as success.

Method and research question

Authors describe 2022–2023 development, one frozen strategy assessed on 2024 A-shares, simulated impact/cash constraints and nine models. They report that none beat an equal-weight market in the sealed year; this is an author-reported negative result, not a verified finding.

Material limitations

True sealing of repeated submissions, selection/leaderboard reuse, input availability, exchange constraints, simulated impact calibration, model exact versions, budget matching and statistical uncertainty are not verified.

How to test the claim

Adopt sealed-period, one-submission, fixed-baseline and action-log checks as a design rubric for any future trading-agent evaluation. Do not infer agent profitability.
